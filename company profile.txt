# McCoy's Doors and Windows (Pty) Ltd

### Bespoke Timber, Aluminium & Glass Solutions Since 1995

**McCoy's Doors and Windows (Pty) Ltd** is a Johannesburg-based manufacturer specialising in the design, manufacture, supply and installation of **premium timber, aluminium and glass doors and windows**.

Established in **1995**, McCoy's has built a reputation for quality workmanship, reliable service and custom-made solutions designed to suit the unique requirements of residential and commercial properties.

From elegant entrance doors and custom timber doors to modern aluminium systems, frameless glass solutions and professional door and window repairs, we provide a complete solution from concept to installation.

---

At McCoy's Doors and Windows, we combine **quality materials, skilled craftsmanship and practical design** to create products that are built to perform while enhancing the appearance of your property.
